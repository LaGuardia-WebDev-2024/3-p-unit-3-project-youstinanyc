cinnamon swirl cookies!!

Recipes by Youstina

  • Time: 25 minutes
  • Servings:8
Ingredients Quantity
butter 1/2 cup
eggs egg+egg yolk
sugar 1 cup
salt a pinch
baking soda a pinch
cinnamon 2 tbsp
vanilla extract 1 tsp

Step 1: Cream the butter and sugar together until combined, then add the eggs and vanilla and stir well.

Step 2:Whisk together the dry ingredients in a separate bowl, then gradually add them to the wet ingredients until everything is combined.

Step 3:Make the cinnamon sugar swirl: whisk together the brown sugar, cinnamon, and salt. Add the butter and mix until you have a paste.

Step 4:Drop the swirl into the dough by large spoonfuls then, carefully, fold it into the dough, mixing until you have a distinct ripple

Step 5:Scoop and roll 1 ½ tablespoon portions of dough between your palms, then place 2″ apart on parchment lined baking sheets.

Step 6:Bake for 10-11 minutes. when let the cookies cool completely on the baking sheets before enjoying!!!

fluffy pumpkin cinnamon rolls!!

  • Time: 2 hours😦
  • Servings:12
    • Ingredients Quantity
    whole milk 1/3 cup
    sugar 1/4 cup
    yeast 2 1/4 tsp
    canned pumpkin 4 tbsp
    butter 5 tbsp
    salt a pinch
    egg 1
    pumpkin spice 1 tbsp
    flour 3 1/4 cups
    filling Ingredients Quantity
    brown sugar 3/4 cup
    pumpkin spice 1 1/2 tbsp
    salt 2 tiny pinch tsp
    butter 8 tbsp
    vanilla glaze Ingredients Quantity
    sugar 1 cup
    butter 1 tbsp
    vanilla extract 1 tsp
    whole milk 2 tbsp
    cinnamon sticks 6

    Step 1: Warm milk to 105-110 degrees F by placing milk in a microwave safe bowl and microwaving it for 30 seconds. Transfer warm milk and sugar to the bowl of a stand mixer or a large mixing bowl and sprinkle yeast on top.

    Step 2:Add the canned pumpkin puree, melted butter, salt and egg, mixing with your stand mixer or a hand mixer until smooth and creamy.

    Step 3:Mix in the pumpkin pie spice and flour until a dough begins to form.

    Step 4:Place the dough hook on your mixer and knead dough on low speed for 8-10 minutes.

    Step 5:Transfer the dough ball to a large bowl greased with nonstick cooking spray and cover with plastic wrap. Allow the dough to rise for 1 hour or until doubled in size.

    Step 6:Bake for 10-11 minutes. when let the cookies cool completely on the baking sheets before enjoying!!!
